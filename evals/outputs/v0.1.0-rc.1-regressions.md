# Regression Evidence — v0.1.0-rc.1 Development

These sanitized outputs are retained as evidence of failures found during forward testing. They are not release-candidate pass outputs and are not counted in the final result.

## Customer-requested date became a company commitment

### Failed output

Subject: Update on the Export Issue

Hi Alice,

I’m sorry the export failed and understand the disruption this has caused.

We’re checking the issue with our technical team now. We’ll share a progress update with you later today and let you know the next steps as soon as we have more information.

If available, please share the approximate time of the failed export, the export format, and a screenshot of any error message. This will help us investigate more quickly.

Best,  
[Name]

### Why it failed

The user supplied the customer's requested update date, not an authorized company commitment. “We’ll share a progress update ... today” converted a request into a promise.

### Fix and verification

The Skill now distinguishes a requested deadline from an authorized company commitment. The fresh-context rerun in `v0.1.0-rc.1-primary.md` preserved the customer's request without promising the date.

## Incident draft invented containment activity

### Representative failed output

BLOCKER — Human review required

需由 Security、Privacy、Legal 及事故负责人审核。当前尚未确认是否发生 API key 泄露、影响范围、事故是否已受控或修复时间。

Draft — not approved or ready to send/publish

**Subject: Important Security Incident Update**

We are investigating a security incident identified last night that may have exposed customer API keys. We have not yet confirmed whether any keys were accessed or disclosed.

As a precaution, we recommend rotating or revoking your API keys and reviewing recent API activity.

We have taken immediate steps to contain the incident and are working to determine its scope.

We will provide our next update within 24 hours, or sooner if material information becomes available.

### Why it failed

“We have taken immediate steps to contain the incident” was not supplied by the user. Three independent runs showed the same category of unsupported containment or remediation language.

### Fix and verification

The Skill now uses an incident fact whitelist. When containment or remediation facts are absent, a draft may state only the investigation, known unknowns, recipient precautions, contact placeholder, and next-update timing. Three fresh independent runs then passed; see the primary and repeated-critical output files.
